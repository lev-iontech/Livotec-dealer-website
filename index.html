01 Why now

The way Filipinos get drinking water is changing.

Households still carry, store and re-order gallons every week. The next step is water that is already there: purified at home, on demand, from an appliance that adds value to the space.

Traditional

  • Gallon delivery
  • Refilling stations
  • Bottled water
  • Repeated carrying and storage

Emerging

  • Water available at home
  • Convenience
  • A premium appliance that adds value
  • Purification plus temperature control

The consumer problem

Water passes through many hands before it reaches the glass. Every step is a chance for quality to change.

  1. 1Water treatment facility
  2. 2Water pipes
  3. 3Household plumbing
  4. 4Faucet
  5. 5Drinking glass
  • Water quality can vary depending on source and distribution.
  • Household plumbing and storage can affect perceived water quality.
  • Consumers still commonly rely on refilled gallon or bottled water.
  • Drinking-water convenience remains fragmented.

The category gap

Gallons solve access. Basic purifiers solve filtration. Nothing on most shelves solves the whole experience. That is the space Livotec fills.

Trust in every drop
